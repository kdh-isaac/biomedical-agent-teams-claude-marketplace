# Immuno-Oncology Failure Modes

- Overstating mouse xenograft or in vitro cytotoxicity as human clinical efficacy.
- Treating bulk tumor signatures as CAR-T-intrinsic state without cell-type resolution.
- Mixing tumor-cell antigen expression, immune-infiltrate abundance, and therapy response endpoints.
- Ignoring antigen escape, tonic signaling, exhaustion, trafficking, suppressive myeloid cells, and cytokine-toxicity tradeoffs.
- Reporting checkpoint, cytokine, or armored-CAR mechanisms without species, model, dose, and payload-context limits.
